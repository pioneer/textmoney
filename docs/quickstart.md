# Quickstart

This guide gets you started with TextMoney on Android or a desktop computer.
No TextMoney sign-up or internet connection is needed to record your finances.

## On a phone (Android)

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/pioneer/textmoney/releases/latest)
   and download the APK for your phone. `arm64-v8a` is right for most modern
   phones. If you are unsure which file to choose, see
   [Android installation notes](platform-notes.md#android).
2. Open the downloaded APK and follow Android's prompts to install TextMoney.
3. Open TextMoney and choose a folder for your records. Use a local folder that
   stays available on your phone; a cloud-only folder is not suitable. Allow
   access when Android asks.

## On a desktop computer (Windows or Linux)

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/pioneer/textmoney/releases/latest)
   and download the file for your computer:
   - **Windows:** Download and open `TextMoney-...-windows-x64-setup.exe`, then
     follow the installer. Open TextMoney from the Start Menu.
   - **Linux:** Download `TextMoney-...-x86_64.AppImage`, make it executable,
     then open it. See [Linux AppImage instructions](platform-notes.md#linux-appimage)
     if you need help making it executable.
2. When TextMoney opens, choose a folder for your records. Keep it somewhere
   you can find again, outside the app's installation folder.

## Create your first records

1. Open **Accounts** (on a phone, tap **More** first), then choose **Create
   account**. For example, create **Cash** or **Bank** and choose its currency.
   An account code is required, but TextMoney suggests one for you; you can
   leave it as is.
   If you want TextMoney to show the money you already have, enter its current
   balance as the optional opening balance.
2. Open **Transactions** and choose **Add transaction**. Enter what you spent
   and how much, then choose the account and an expense category. Choose an
   existing category or create one if needed, then save.
3. Your balance and transaction appear in the app. You can add income,
   transfers, budgets and other details later.

## Optional: use your records on another device

You can use TextMoney on just one device; synchronization is not required.
To use the same records on multiple devices, follow
[Synchronization](synchronization.md) before setting it up. TextMoney does not
sync files itself: you need an external sync app to sync the whole records
folder. Wait for it to finish before editing on another device, and edit on
only one device at a time.
